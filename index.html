Laying our cards on the table

Fifty-two years in, we’re still dealing.

Our very first issue, in October 1974, asked a question on its cover: Power in Dallas — who holds the cards? Fifty-two years and 52 weeks of research later, this year’s Best of Big D answers with a deck of our own.

Four suits, one for each way we love this city. Every card is a winner, picked by our editors. Some got the full photo-shoot treatment; all of them earned their spot.
